Research date: 2026-11-12. Scope: an unmodified Nintendo Switch 2 sending live gameplay directly over USB to macOS, without a dock, capture card, or console modification.

## Decision

**BLOCKED / UNVERIFIED:** no console USB descriptors, endpoint traffic, or attributable live gameplay frames have been collected for this investigation. LEA-8 must provide the physical USB baseline before the exposed interfaces can be classified. **NO-GO for committing to a direct USB live-capture product now**, because its required evidence is missing.
